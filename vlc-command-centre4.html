Connect to Google Sheet
This connects the dialler directly to your Lead Tracker sheet. Once connected, marking a call status or adding notes updates the sheet automatically — no manual copying needed.
1 Open the Apps Script file provided to you (VLC-AppsScript-Sync.gs)
2 Open your Google Sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script
3 Paste the script code, save, then click Deploy → New deployment → Web app
4 Set "Who has access" to Anyone, click Deploy, authorize
5 Copy the Web App URL it gives you and paste it below
