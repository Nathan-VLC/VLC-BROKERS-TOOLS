Scenario 1

General Introduction — Cold Outreach

Use when reaching out to a business owner with no prior contact.
WhatsApp Message
Hi [Name],

My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. We are a business funding brokerage based in Cape Town, operating nationally.

We assist SME business owners in accessing short-term loans, bank overdrafts, medium-term loans, and asset finance — quickly and without upfront fees or payments.

I would love to find out if funding could help your business right now. Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call this week?

No obligation at all.
Email Version
Subject: Business Funding for [Business Name] — No Upfront Fees

Hi [Name],

I hope this message finds you well.

My name is [Broker Name] and I am a funding broker at Vital Link Capital, a Cape Town-based brokerage that assists South African SMEs in accessing business finance.

We work with a panel of lenders to source short-term loans, bank overdraft facilities, medium-term loans, and asset finance. Our service involves no upfront fees or payments — we are only compensated once funding is successfully arranged.

If your business could benefit from additional working capital, equipment finance, or a credit facility, I would welcome the opportunity to explore the options available to you.

Would you be available for a brief call this week?

Kind regards,
[Broker Name]
Vital Link Capital
[Phone Number]
Scenario 2

Gatekeeper — Reaching the Decision Maker

Use when a receptionist or assistant answers. Goal is to get through to the owner or financial manager.
WhatsApp — After Gatekeeper Blocks You
Hi [Name],

I called earlier and spoke to your office. My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital.

We assist business owners in accessing funding — short-term loans, overdraft facilities, and asset finance — with no upfront fees or payments required.

I wanted to reach out directly in case there is an opportunity to support your business. Would you be open to a quick call at a time that suits you?
Phone Script — Speaking to Gatekeeper
"Good morning / afternoon. My name is [Broker Name] calling from Vital Link Capital. I am looking to speak with the owner or financial manager regarding business funding options. Could you let me know who the best person would be to speak to, and if they are available?"

[If asked what it is regarding:]
"We are a business funding brokerage. We help SME business owners access loans and credit facilities quickly and without upfront fees. I just need five minutes of their time."
Scenario 3

After Call — Follow Up Message

Send immediately after an initial phone call where the prospect showed interest.
WhatsApp — After Positive Call
Hi [Name],

Thank you for your time just now.

As discussed, I am [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. We will explore the best funding options available for [Business Name] and come back to you with what is accessible.

To get started I will need the following documents:
• 3 months recent bank statements
• Latest management accounts or financials (if available)
• Copy of your ID
• Proof of business registration

Please send these through when you are ready and I will get the process moving for you.

No upfront fees or payments at any stage.
WhatsApp — After No Answer / Callback Request
Hi [Name],

I tried to reach you earlier. My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital — we are a business funding brokerage operating nationally.

We assist SME owners in accessing short-term loans, overdraft facilities, and asset finance with no upfront fees or payments.

Please let me know a good time to connect and I will call you back.
Scenario 4

Referral — Warm Introduction

Use when reaching out to someone referred by an existing client, accountant, or partner.
WhatsApp — Referral Outreach
Hi [Name],

My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. [Referrer Name] suggested I reach out to you directly.

We are a business funding brokerage and we helped [Referrer Name / their business] access funding recently. I wanted to reach out to see if there is anything we could do for your business as well.

We work with a wide panel of lenders and assist with short-term loans, overdraft facilities, medium-term loans, and asset finance. No upfront fees or payments at any stage.

Would you be open to a quick call this week?
