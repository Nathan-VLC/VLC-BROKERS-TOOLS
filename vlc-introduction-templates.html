Introduction Templates

WhatsApp and email introduction scripts across all product lines. Click Copy to paste directly.

Scenario 1
General Introduction: Cold Outreach
Use when reaching out to a business owner with no prior contact.

WhatsApp Message

Hi [Name], My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. We are a business funding brokerage based in Cape Town, operating nationally. We assist SME business owners in accessing short-term loans, bank overdrafts, medium-term loans, and asset finance, quickly and without upfront fees or payments. I would love to find out if funding could help your business right now. Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call this week? No obligation at all.

Email Version

Subject: Business Funding for [Business Name]: No Upfront Fees Hi [Name], I hope this message finds you well. My name is [Broker Name] and I am a funding broker at Vital Link Capital, a Cape Town-based brokerage that assists South African SMEs in accessing business finance. We work with a panel of lenders to source short-term loans, bank overdraft facilities, medium-term loans, and asset finance. Our service involves no upfront fees or payments. We are only compensated once funding is successfully arranged. If your business could benefit from additional working capital, equipment finance, or a credit facility, I would welcome the opportunity to explore the options available to you. Would you be available for a brief call this week? Kind regards, [Broker Name] Vital Link Capital [Phone Number]
Scenario 2
Gatekeeper: Reaching the Decision Maker
Use when a receptionist or assistant answers. Goal is to get through to the owner or financial manager.

WhatsApp — After Gatekeeper Blocks You

Hi [Name], I called earlier and spoke to your office. My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. We assist business owners in accessing funding, including short-term loans, overdraft facilities, and asset finance, with no upfront fees or payments required. I wanted to reach out directly in case there is an opportunity to support your business. Would you be open to a quick call at a time that suits you?

Phone Script — Speaking to Gatekeeper

"Good morning / afternoon. My name is [Broker Name] calling from Vital Link Capital. I am looking to speak with the owner or financial manager regarding business funding options. Could you let me know who the best person would be to speak to, and if they are available?" [If asked what it is regarding:] "We are a business funding brokerage. We help SME business owners access loans and credit facilities quickly and without upfront fees. I just need five minutes of their time."
Scenario 3
After Call: Follow Up Message
Send immediately after an initial phone call where the prospect showed interest.

WhatsApp — After Positive Call

Hi [Name], Thank you for your time just now. As discussed, I am [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. We will explore the best funding options available for [Business Name] and come back to you with what is accessible. To get started I will need the following documents: 1. 3 months recent bank statements 2. Latest management accounts or financials (if available) 3. Copy of your ID 4. Proof of business registration Please send these through when you are ready and I will get the process moving for you. No upfront fees or payments at any stage.

WhatsApp — After No Answer / Callback Request

Hi [Name], I tried to reach you earlier. My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. We are a business funding brokerage operating nationally. We assist SME owners in accessing short-term loans, overdraft facilities, and asset finance with no upfront fees or payments. Please let me know a good time to connect and I will call you back.

Scenario 6

Use when approaching SME owners who may need immediate access to capital, an ongoing credit facility, or a review of their business insurance. Position VLC as a single point of contact across all three. Funding and protection in one conversation.

WhatsApp — Short Term Loan Focus

Hi [Name], My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. We are a business funding brokerage operating nationally across South Africa. We assist established SMEs in accessing short-term business loans — typically between 3 and 12 months — for working capital, stock purchasing, or cash flow management. No upfront fees or payments at any stage. Would you be open to a quick call this week to find out what is available for [Business Name]?

WhatsApp — Bank Overdraft or Credit Facility Focus

Hi [Name], My name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. We assist South African SMEs in accessing bank overdraft facilities and revolving credit lines. A business overdraft or credit facility gives you ongoing access to funds without having to reapply each time. We work with a panel of banks and alternative lenders to find the right fit for your business profile. No upfront fees or payments at any stage. Would you be open to a quick call this week?
