Client Business Details

Fuel / Transport Operation

Funding Requirement

Why Fuel and Transport Clients Need Funding

  • Fuel must be purchased upfront. Cash flow gaps between buying and selling fuel are common across retailers and distributors.
  • Fleet expansion or replacement requires significant capital outlay, often beyond what retained earnings can cover.
  • Maintenance costs are high and often unplanned, disrupting operations and income.
  • Depot and storage facility setup requires large upfront investment.
  • Seasonal demand spikes require stock and capacity ahead of income.

Best Funding Products for This Sector

Short-term loan (3 to 6 months)

Best suited for fuel float, stock purchases, and emergency repairs where quick turnaround is needed.

Asset finance

Vehicle and truck acquisition, tankers, forklifts, and heavy equipment. Requires a supplier quote or proforma invoice.

Medium-term loan (12 to 36 months)

Fleet expansion, depot development, and larger capital requirements with structured repayments.

Overdraft facility

Ongoing working capital for fuel resellers and distributors managing recurring cash flow gaps.

Key Qualification Criteria

  • Minimum 12 months in operation. Preferred 2 or more years for bank products.
  • Active business bank account showing regular monthly turnover.
  • Monthly turnover of at least R100 000 for most short-term products.
  • Clean or manageable credit profile preferred. Adverse listings may limit options.
  • For asset finance: supplier quote or proforma invoice required before submission.
