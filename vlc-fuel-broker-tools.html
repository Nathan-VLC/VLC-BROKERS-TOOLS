Why Fuel and Transport Clients Need Funding

  • Fuel must be purchased upfront — cash flow gaps between buying and selling fuel are common
  • Fleet expansion or replacement requires significant capital outlay
  • Maintenance costs are high and often unplanned
  • Depot and storage facility setup requires large investment
  • Seasonal demand spikes require stock and capacity ahead of income

Best Funding Products for This Sector

  • Short-term loan (3 to 6 months): Fuel float, stock purchases, emergency repairs
  • Asset finance: Vehicle and truck acquisition, tankers, equipment
  • Medium-term loan (12 to 36 months): Fleet expansion, depot development
  • Overdraft facility: Ongoing working capital for fuel resellers and distributors

Key Qualification Criteria

  • Minimum 12 months in operation (preferred 2 plus years for bank products)
  • Active business bank account showing regular turnover
  • Monthly turnover of at least R100,000 for most short-term products
  • Clean or manageable credit profile preferred
  • For asset finance — supplier quote or proforma invoice required
