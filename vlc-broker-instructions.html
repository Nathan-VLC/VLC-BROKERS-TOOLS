Broker Instructions

Full 10-step broker process with rules, tools guidance, and WhatsApp message templates.

1

Identify and Contact the Prospect

Cold Call / Outreach

Use the lead tracker to identify your prospects. Call first — WhatsApp or email follows if no answer.

  • Aim for a minimum of 20 calls per day per broker
  • Best call times: 8:00–10:00 and 14:00–16:30 SAST
  • Always aim to speak to the owner or financial decision-maker directly
  • If gatekeeper answers — use the gatekeeper script (see Cold Call Script tool)
WhatsApp — No Answer Follow Up
Hi [Name], my name is [Broker Name] from Vital Link Capital. I tried reaching you earlier. We assist SME business owners in accessing funding — short-term loans, overdraft facilities, and asset finance. No upfront fees or payments at any stage. Please let me know a good time to connect.
2

Qualify the Prospect

Discovery Call

During the first call, qualify the prospect before investing time in an application.

  • How long has the business been operating? (Minimum 1 year for most products)
  • What is the average monthly turnover?
  • What is the funding requirement and purpose?
  • Does the business have an active business bank account?
  • Any existing loans or judgements? (Important for credit assessment)

Do not submit applications for businesses under 6 months old, with turnover under R50,000 per month, or with clear credit issues before discussing with Nathan.

3

Send the Introduction Message

WhatsApp / Email

Once qualified, send the appropriate introduction template immediately after the call.

  • Use the Introduction Templates tool to copy the correct scenario
  • Always personalise with the business name and owner name
  • Confirm: no upfront fees, no payments before funding is received
4

Request Documents

Document Collection

Use the Document Request Templates tool to send the correct request message based on the product type.

  • Short-term loan: 3 months bank statements, ID copy, proof of business registration
  • Bank overdraft / medium-term: 6 months bank statements, latest financials or management accounts, ID, registration docs
  • Asset finance: Quote for asset, 3 months statements, ID, registration

Do not submit incomplete applications. Missing documents cause delays and reflect poorly on the brokerage. Always confirm documents are received before proceeding.

5

Check Affordability

Calculator

Before submitting, run the Affordability Calculator to confirm the deal makes sense.

  • Enter the average monthly turnover from bank statements
  • Check the Affordable / Marginal / Not Affordable verdict
  • Marginal deals can still be submitted but manage client expectations
  • Do not submit Not Affordable deals without speaking to Nathan first
6

Complete the Funding Application Form

Client Document

Get the client to complete the VLC Funding Application Form before submission to any lender.

  • Send the form link to the client or complete it together on the call
  • Confirm all fields are complete — especially business registration number, bank details, and required amount
  • Attach the signed form to the submission
7

Submit to Lenders

Submission

Submit the complete application pack to the appropriate lender(s) based on the product and client profile.

  • Short-term loans: submit to 2 to 3 lenders simultaneously for best offer
  • Bank products: submit to the client's primary bank first, then alternatives
  • Update the lead tracker status to "Submitted" immediately after sending
  • Note the lender name and submission date in the tracker
8

Follow Up with Lenders

Pipeline Management

Do not wait for lenders to come back to you. Follow up proactively.

  • Short-term lenders: follow up after 24 hours if no response
  • Bank products: follow up every 48 to 72 hours
  • Keep the client updated at every stage — silence kills deals
  • Update the tracker status as the deal progresses
9

Present the Offer to the Client

Offer Stage

Once an offer is received, present it to the client clearly and professionally.

  • Confirm: loan amount, term, repayment amount, and total cost of credit
  • Explain the repayment structure — daily, weekly, or monthly deductions
  • If client accepts, update the tracker to "Offer Accepted"
  • Obtain signed mandate letter if not already in place
WhatsApp — Offer Received
Hi [Name], good news — we have received an offer for [Business Name].

The details are as follows:
• Loan amount: R[Amount]
• Term: [X] months
• Repayment: R[Amount] per [day/week/month]

Please let me know if you would like to proceed and I will get the paperwork moving.
10

Close and Record the Deal

Close

Once funding is paid out, complete the closing process.

  • Confirm payout with the lender and client
  • Update the tracker status to "Funded" with the payout date and commission amount
  • Request a referral from the client — this is the best time to ask
  • Send a brief thank you message to the client
WhatsApp — Payout Confirmation
Hi [Name], congratulations — funding for [Business Name] has been paid out.

It was a pleasure assisting you. If you know any other business owners who may benefit from funding, please do not hesitate to refer them to us.

We are always here when you need us again.
