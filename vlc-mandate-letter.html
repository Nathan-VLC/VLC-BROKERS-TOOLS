I, _______________________, duly authorised representative of _______________________ (Registration No: _______________), hereby mandate Vital Link Capital (Pty) Ltd to act as my authorised funding broker and to approach lenders and financial institutions on my behalf for the purpose of sourcing business finance.

No Upfront Fees or Payments. No fees or payments are required from the client at any stage prior to funding being received. Vital Link Capital is compensated by the lender upon successful arrangement of finance.

This mandate authorises Vital Link Capital to:

  • Submit my business and financial information to appropriate lenders and financial institutions;
  • Obtain credit reports and conduct assessments necessary for funding applications;
  • Negotiate terms and present funding offers on my behalf;
  • Communicate with lenders throughout the application and approval process.

This mandate remains valid for a period of 90 days from the date of signature, or until funding has been successfully arranged, whichever occurs first. The client may withdraw this mandate at any time in writing.

All information provided to Vital Link Capital will be treated as confidential and will only be shared with relevant lenders and financial institutions for the purpose of sourcing funding as described herein.
