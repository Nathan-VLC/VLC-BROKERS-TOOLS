WhatsApp — Bank Facility Document Request
Hi [Name],

To get the ball rolling on your bank facility application, the banks require a more complete document pack. Please send through the following:

1. 6 months most recent business bank statements (PDF)
2. Latest annual financial statements or management accounts (within 12 months)
3. Copy of your ID document
4. Proof of business registration (CIPC certificate)
5. Proof of business address (utility bill or lease agreement)
6. Company resolution or letter of authority (if applicable)

The more complete the pack, the faster the bank can assess your application.

No upfront fees or payments at any stage. Send to this WhatsApp or email to [email address].

Hi [Name],

• Company resolution authorising the application
• Lease agreement or property ownership documents

WhatsApp — Asset Finance Document Request
Hi [Name],

To proceed with your asset finance application, please send through the following:

1. Supplier quote or proforma invoice for the asset (with description and price)
2. 3 months most recent business bank statements (PDF)
3. Copy of your ID document
4. Proof of business registration (CIPC certificate)

If the asset is second-hand, please also include:
• Proof of ownership from the seller
• Valuation or market-related price confirmation

No upfront fees or payments required. Please send to this WhatsApp or email to [email address].
