Saving Changes to Google Sheet

Changes made in the dialler (call status and notes) are saved locally on your device. To update the Google Sheet:

Option 1 — Update sheet directly

Open the Google Sheet on your desktop and update the Call Status and Call Date columns manually based on your call outcomes.

Option 2 — WhatsApp automation (coming soon)

Once your WhatsApp Cloud API and Make.com automation are set up, call outcomes will sync to the sheet automatically.
